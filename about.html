Meet the Founders

Founders

Srividya Murugan and Jeyashree Murugan, sisters and founders of Third Space 360, bring a shared sensitivity towards spaces, details, art, and the quiet ways environments shape everyday living.

Their vision is rooted in a simple belief: a well-designed space should go beyond appearance, supporting how people live, work, move, and feel. For them, meaningful spaces are shaped through function, proportion, material sensitivity, craftsmanship, and lived experience.

With multidisciplinary exposure, creative instinct, and a family background shaped by decades of execution legacy through Corrosion Control Systems — CCS, they bring both imagination and practical understanding to design.

Through Third Space 360, they aim to create residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces shaped by intention, detail, and disciplined execution.
